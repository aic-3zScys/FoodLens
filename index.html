☠️ Banned & Non-Food Substances Found in India

⚠️ DANGER ZONE

These are substances that are strictly prohibited in food by FSSAI and other regulators. They are industrial chemicals, textile dyes, or fertilizers that have no place in food. Their presence indicates adulteration. If you spot any of these on a label or suspect them in a product, do not buy or consume it — and report it to FSSAI.
